Privacy

Pursae Analytics sets no cookies and stores nothing in the browser. A visitor is counted with a hash of the IP address and the browser, mixed with a secret that is replaced every day and then deleted, so nobody — not even you — can follow a visitor from one day to the next or find out who they are. IP addresses are never stored. That is why no consent banner is needed for it. Countries are shown when your proxy or CDN reports them (Cloudflare CF-IPCountry, CloudFront, Vercel or an X-Country-Code header).

Delete statistics
