# Oak & Form

An airy Scandinavian furniture store for Goocommerce: a quiet header (logo, centred menu, text links for
search, account and cart with a mini-cart), a framed hero with a linen caption card, tall staggered category
panels, calm product cards on a birch backdrop whose add-to-cart button slides up on hover, a gallery-led
product page with a sticky summary, and a light footer with a large wordmark. On small screens the menu
becomes a drawer from the left.

**Customize › Colours & Type**: accent, wood tone, background and text colours, font pairing (Albert Sans
with light headings, or Gilda Display headings), button shape (softly rounded, square, pill), evening mode.
**Customize › Header & Store**: announcement bar and its text, header layout (split or stacked), products
per row, product image backdrop.
**Customize › Footer**: the large wordmark, footer text.

The demo content imports a complete store: twelve pieces of furniture, lighting and decor in four
categories (a sofa with a fabric choice, a sale lounge chair), reviews, a welcome coupon and shipping,
plus a home page, About, Contact and a Journal.

Fonts: Albert Sans and Gilda Display (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations.
