# Greenleaf Market

A friendly organic grocery and farm shop theme. Fresh greens and tomato red on kraft paper, Bitter slab headings and Caveat hand-lettered labels.

## Highlights

- Announcement bar, green header with a scalloped edge and an order button
- Split hero with a polaroid-framed image and a 'Grown local' stamp
- Weekly box on a chalkboard (`chalkboard` with a `price-list`), price tags (`price-tag`, `price-from`), producer cards
- Masonry, grid or list blog with slightly tilted paper cards, optional paper texture
- Chalkboard footer with address and shop hours
- Full width with banner page template (`page-full`) for the weekly box

## Customizer

- **Colors & Type**: Leaf green, tomato accent, kraft paper, typefaces, paper fibre texture
- **Header & Announcement**: Show the announcement bar, announcement, box button label, box button link
- **Front Page Hero**: Hero image, handwritten line, heading, text
- **Blog & Footer**: Blog layout, show the sidebar beside posts, shop hours, address, footer text

## Demo content

Seven recipe and news posts (squash soup, November produce, refills, the baker, strawberry jam, the Saturday market, five salads), a designed Home page, The weekly box, Our producers, About and Visit us; main and social menus, sidebar and footer widgets. All text and images are original; the illustrations were generated for this theme.
