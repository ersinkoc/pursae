# Encore

A loud, dark theme for bands and musicians: a full-screen hero with a neon-glow title and a
scrolling ticker, tour dates and releases designed as page content, news cards with big date
stamps, a gallery and a sign-up band, in condensed display type with a neon accent and optional
film grain.

**Category:** Music · **Style:** Dark stage with neon accent

## Customizer

- **Colours & Effects:** neon accent, second accent and stage colour (live preview), neon glow,
  film grain, font pairing (Anton & Space Grotesk, Archivo Condensed, system).
- **Layout:** news layout (grid or wide list), sticky header, scrolling ticker.
- **Hero & Tickets:** hero heading, hero line, hero image, ticket button label and link, footer text.

## Designed sections

Group blocks with these classes are styled: `ec-section`, `ec-tour` containing `ec-show` rows
(paragraphs `ec-show__date`, `ec-show__city`, `ec-show__venue` and a button; add `is-sold-out` to
strike a show through), `ec-releases` (album covers), `ec-latest` on a Latest Posts block,
`ec-gallery` and `ec-signup` (full-width band). Paragraphs with `ec-kicker` become small labels.

## Demo content

"Neon Harbour", a synth-rock band: seven news, tour and studio posts, a designed Home page with
tour dates, releases, news and a gallery, plus News, Tour, Music, The Band (with a Gallery
sub-page) and Contact. Text and images are original.
