# Bright Smile Dental

A cheerful, rounded theme for dental clinics: fresh aqua and white with a coral accent, soft bubbly shapes, a wavy hero with booking buttons, treatments, the team, before-and-after results, reviews and oral-care articles.

Category: **health** · Style: Fresh aqua and white, rounded and playful

## Highlights

- A wavy aqua hero with a blob-framed image, booking buttons and badges (comma-separated)
- Treatment cards with round icons, round team portraits, before/after images and review cards
- A booking band above the footer with the phone number
- Corner roundness (soft, round, extra round) and optional floating bubbles
- Header as a floating pill, a full-width white bar or an aqua bar
- Responsive down to 360px with an accessible mobile menu (Escape closes it), visible focus states and `prefers-reduced-motion` support

## Customizer

**Colors & Shapes**: Primary (aqua); Accent (coral); Text and headings; Soft background; Corner roundness; Floating bubbles in the hero and booking band; Typefaces.

**Header & Booking**: Header style; Keep the header visible while scrolling; Show opening hours and phone above the header; Opening hours; Phone number; Booking button label; Booking button link; Booking band heading.

**Front Page Hero**: Hero image; Small line above the heading; Heading; Text; Second button label; Second button link; Badges under the buttons.

**Articles & Footer**: Post list layout; Sidebar; Show featured images; Show the author on posts and cards; Footer text.

## Demo content

Appearance › Themes › Import Demo Content adds 8 posts, 6 pages (Gentle dentistry for the whole family, Smile tips, Treatments, Meet the team, About the clinic, Book a visit), 4 categories, 12 images, a main menu with submenus, a footer menu, sidebar and footer widgets and the Customizer values shown in the screenshot. The front page is a static page built from blocks; the template `page-wide` is used for full-width pages. All names, firms, addresses and numbers are fictional.
