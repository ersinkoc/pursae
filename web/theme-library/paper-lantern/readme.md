# Paper Lantern

A warm, cosy personal journal theme for WordPress Go. One comfortable column on cream paper,
handwritten accents in Caveat, rounded Nunito for reading, polaroid photos held on with washi
tape and a gently swaying string of lanterns over the masthead.

Made for diaries, family notes, kitchen and garden logs, letters and slow-living blogs.

## Features

- Single, comfortable reading column with three widths.
- Three entry list styles: paper cards, diary pages with big handwritten dates in the margin, or a compact ruled list.
- Three photo styles: polaroid with tape, soft rounded corners, or plain.
- Three font pairings: Cosy (Caveat + Nunito), Storybook (Caveat + Lora) and Plain (system fonts).
- A greeting note and an optional welcome picture on the first page of the journal.
- Footer notes widget area (up to three columns) and a footer links menu on a kraft-paper footer.
- Accessible: skip link, visible dashed focus rings, keyboard-friendly submenus, reduced motion respected.
- Self-hosted fonts (SIL Open Font License), no external requests.

## Customizer

**Paper & ink** — accent color, paper color, ink color, paper grain texture.
**Journal layout** — font pairing, entry list style, photo style, column width, show the author's name.
**Masthead & footer** — string of lanterns, welcome note, greeting, welcome picture, footer note.

## Menus and widgets

- *Primary Menu* — under the site title (submenus open on hover and focus; a toggle on phones).
- *Footer Links* — small pill links in the footer.
- *Footer notes* — widget area shown in the footer.

## Demo content

The demo is a fictional slow-living journal, "The Lantern Notebook": eight entries about the
kitchen, the garden, walks, handmade things and letters, with original illustrations generated
for this theme.
