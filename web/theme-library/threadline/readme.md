# Threadline

A calm, minimal apparel boutique for Goocommerce: an announcement bar, a centred logo between the menu and the
account and cart icons with a mini-cart, an editorial hero, category tiles, generous product grids, quiet
product pages and a focused two-column checkout.

**Customize › Colours & Type**: accent, background and text colours, font pairing, rounded or square buttons.
**Customize › Header & Store**: announcement bar, logo position, products per row, sticky header, footer text.

The demo content imports a complete store: twelve products (with sizes and colours), four categories,
reviews, a welcome coupon and shipping, plus a home page, About, Contact and a Journal.

Fonts: Instrument Serif and Hanken Grotesk (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations.
