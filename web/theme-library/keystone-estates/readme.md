# Keystone Estates

A clean real estate agency theme in forest green and warm stone, set in Manrope. Listings are ordinary posts: categories give the status (For Sale, For Rent, Sold), tags the neighbourhood, and the excerpt the price line shown on cards.

## Highlights

- Search-first hero with a large search box and "popular searches" chips from the Hero menu location
- Header that floats transparently over the hero on the front page (or stays solid), optional sticky header
- Listing cards with status badges, neighbourhood pins and price lines; grid or wide-row layouts
- Listing styles: `listing-price`, a `property-facts` grid, a `feature-list` with ticks and an `agent-card`
- Home page blocks: `stats`, neighbourhood `area-tiles` / `area-tile`, `service-box`
- Office details in the footer, footer menu and widgets

## Customizer

- **Colors & Type**: primary green, accent, stone background, typefaces
- **Header & Hero**: header style on the front page, sticky header, header button, hero image, heading, text, search placeholder, image darkening
- **Listings & Footer**: listing layout, sidebar, office details, footer text

## Demo content

Seven listings (houses, a cottage, a townhouse, apartments for rent, a lakeside cabin, a sold home) and two market articles; Home, Listings, About, Selling with us and Contact pages; main, hero and footer menus, sidebar and footer widgets. All text and images are original.
