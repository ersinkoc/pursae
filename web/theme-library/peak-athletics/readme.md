# Peak Athletics

An energetic sports and outdoor store for Pursae Commerce: a top bar, a bold carbon header with a three-level
mega menu (sports, then products), an inline product search and a volt cart button with a mini-cart; a
full-bleed hero with a volt slash and a stats band; slanted category tiles; product cards that lift on
hover and reveal a full-width add-to-cart bar; a chunky product page with a big volt add-to-cart button;
and a carbon footer with a headline.

**Customize › Colours & Type**: volt accent, signal (sale) colour, background and text colours, headline
style (condensed Big Shoulders or Barlow Extra Bold Italic), button shape (square, slanted, rounded),
dark storefront.
**Customize › Header & Store**: header colour (carbon, light, volt), top bar and its text, inline search,
products per row.
**Customize › Footer**: footer headline and footer text.

The demo content imports a complete store: twelve products in running, training, outdoor and team sports
(a trail shoe with sizes and colours), reviews, coupons and shipping, plus a home page, About, Contact
and a Journal.

Fonts: Big Shoulders Display and Barlow (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations.
