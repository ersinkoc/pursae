# Wanderline

An adventurous travel blog theme for WordPress Go: full-bleed landscape heroes, destination tiles,
postcard story cards with stamp labels, dashed route lines and contour-map accents, in a sunset palette.

## Highlights

- Front-page hero with your own image, title, text, button and a map-style coordinate label.
- The header floats over the hero and turns solid as you scroll (or choose solid / light).
- Story layouts: postcard tiles (first story large), a three-column grid or a wide list.
- Poster (Anton + Karla), journal (serif) or clean (Karla) typography.
- Sidebar left, right or off; footer widget columns and a social links menu.

## Customizer

- **Wanderline Colors**: sunset accent, dusk (header and footer), page background — live preview.
- **Wanderline Layout**: story layout, sidebar, header style, typography, sticky header, featured images, author.
- **Wanderline Hero & Footer**: hero image, small line, title, text, button label and link, map coordinates, footer text.

## Demo content

Eight travel stories across six destination categories, a designed home page with destination tiles,
Destinations, About and Contact pages, a main menu with submenus, a social menu and sidebar/footer widgets.
All text and images are original; the images are generated artwork.
