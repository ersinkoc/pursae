# Pageturner

A literary theme for authors and book bloggers. The author's name is set large in Libre Caslon
Display over small-caps navigation between double rules, with Spectral for reading. The front page
shows the new book as a three-dimensional cover, a wooden bookshelf of covers, reading notes and a
table of signings; the journal lists numbered entries with italic initials and bookmark ribbons,
and every page ends with a newsletter "letter" in the footer. A night-reading mode turns the paper dark.

## Customizer

- **Paper & Ink**: accent ink (oxblood), second ink (slate teal), paper and text colors (live), night reading mode.
- **Layout & Type**: journal layout (numbered entries, cards or a list with covers), sidebar right,
  left or none, typography (Caslon and Spectral, Spectral throughout or system serif), reading size,
  drop caps, sticky header, featured images, author name.
- **The New Book & Newsletter**: the announcement above the header and its link, the book cover
  image, kicker, title, blurb and two buttons on the front page, the newsletter title, text and link,
  and the footer text.

## Demo content

One click imports the site of an invented novelist: a designed home page with a bookshelf of four
original covers, the journal (eight posts: reading notes, writing life, a tour diary, an excerpt, a
reading list and a Q&A, with comments), Books with a child page for the new novel, Events, About and
Contact, menus, widgets and Customizer settings. All text and artwork are original.

Fonts: Libre Caslon Display and Spectral, SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted.
