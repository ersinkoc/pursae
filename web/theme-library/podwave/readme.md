# Podwave

A bright, rounded podcast theme for WordPress Go.

- An animated equaliser logo mark, a pill menu and a pulsing "Subscribe" button in the header.
- A hero with square show art, an ON AIR badge and a waveform band; a "latest episode" player card, host cards, stats and a "listen anywhere" panel on the front page.
- Episode rows with cover art and play buttons (or a cards grid), show-notes pages with a decorative player, chapter timestamps (`pw-timestamps` list with `<code>` times), guest cards (`pw-guest`) and link boxes (`pw-links`).
- A `page-wide` template for the guest wall.

## Customizer

**Colours & Shapes**: violet accent, coral, background, ink, waveform motifs, corners (pills, soft, square), font pairing (Sora & Work Sans, Work Sans, system).
**Episodes & Layout**: rows or cards, sidebar, host names, sticky header.
**Show & Subscribe**: subscribe label and link, show artwork, hero kicker and heading, play button label and link, footer text.

## Demo content

Eight episodes with show notes, a designed home page, Hosts, Guests, Subscribe and Contact. All text and artwork are original; the show and its guests are fictional.

Fonts: Sora and Work Sans (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted.
