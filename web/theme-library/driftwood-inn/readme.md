# Driftwood Inn

A calm boutique hotel theme for a small inn by the sea. Sandy neutrals, ocean blue and driftwood brown, Gilda Display headings with Libre Franklin text.

## Highlights

- Full-screen or medium hero with a transparent header over it and a stay bar (arrival times, rate line, booking button)
- Room cards with rates (`room-card`, `rate`), amenities list with wave bullets (`amenities`)
- Local guide as a magazine (first post large), an even grid or a list
- Inline or centered header, booking button everywhere
- Deep-sea footer with the inn's address, widgets and social links
- Full width with banner page template (`page-full`) for Rooms and Book a stay

## Customizer

- **Colors & Type**: Ocean blue, sand background, driftwood, typefaces
- **Header & Booking**: Header layout, transparent header over the front page hero, booking button label, booking link
- **Front Page Hero**: Hero image, heading, text, hero height, show the stay bar under the hero, arrival times, rate line
- **Local Guide & Footer**: Local guide layout, show the sidebar beside posts, address, footer text

## Demo content

Six local guide posts (a slow day at the point, rock pools, where to eat, autumn swimming, a dunes walk, the new suite), a designed Home page, Rooms & suites with an Amenities sub-page, Book a stay, Our story and Contact; main and social menus, sidebar and footer widgets. All text and images are original; the illustrations were generated for this theme.
