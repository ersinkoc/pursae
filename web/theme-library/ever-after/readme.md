# Ever After

A romantic theme for weddings and celebrations: blush and sage, Cormorant Garamond with Great Vibes script accents.

## Highlights

- An arched hero photo with your names in script, the date and venue in small capitals and an RSVP button.
- Centered header with your names and the date, or a minimal bar; an RSVP button on every page.
- Floral sprig dividers (on or off), a schedule timeline, detail cards and a gallery in the demo.
- Journal layouts: masonry, even grid or a centered list; a blush footer that signs off "With love".

## Customizer

- **Ever After Colors**: rose, blush, sage and page background — live preview.
- **Ever After Layout**: header, journal layout, sidebar, typography, floral dividers, sticky header, featured images, author.
- **Wedding Details**: names, line above the names, date, venue, RSVP label and link, hero image, footer text.

## Demo content

Eight journal posts (our story, planning, guest info, wedding party), a designed home page, Our Story (with Gallery),
Schedule (with Travel & Stay) and RSVP pages, main and footer menus and widgets. The couple and places are fictional;
all text and images are original.
