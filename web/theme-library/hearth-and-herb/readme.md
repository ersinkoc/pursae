# Hearth & Herb

A rustic home-cooking blog theme for families who cook every night: herb green and wood tones on linen, sturdy slab headings and big food photography.

## Features

- Green utility bar with recipe search, plank-stripe dividers and a sticky header
- Recipe list with a big lead recipe (or an even grid, or a list), category tags and “Get the recipe” links
- Printable recipe cards (`recipe-card` group) with a facts row, tick-box ingredients, numbered steps, a cook's note and a print button (link to `#print`); print styles keep just the recipe
- Optional banner photo behind archive and search titles, tip boxes, meal-plan tables and category tiles for pages
- Sidebar right, left or off; accessible focus states; reduced motion; responsive down to 360px

## Customizer

- **Colors & Type** — herb green, wood, linen background, text; three type pairings
- **Layout** — lead/grid/list, sidebar position, photo shape, featured images, cook's name, sticky header
- **Header & Footer** — top bar text, archive banner photo, footer text

## Demo content

Import it from Appearance › Themes: eight recipes from soups to crumble, a recipe index, a weekly meal plan, About and Contact pages, menus, widgets and comments. The blog stays on the front page.

All demo text is original and every demo image is original generated artwork, free to use with the theme.
