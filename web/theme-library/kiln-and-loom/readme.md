# Kiln & Loom

A warm, editorial handmade-crafts market for Pursae Commerce: a market line, a masthead with a serif wordmark,
an italic tagline and stitched rules (with search, account and a basket with a mini-cart), a split hero with
words on sand beside the picture and a maker's stamp, category tiles with indigo swing tags, product cards
that stitch a dashed outline and lift the add-to-basket button on hover, a story-led product page and an
indigo footer with a headline.

**Customize › Colours & Type**: terracotta accent, indigo and olive, background and text colours, font
pairing (Fraunces or Lora with Figtree), button shape (soft, pill, square), paper grain.
**Customize › Masthead & Market**: market line and its text, masthead layout (editorial or compact),
products per row, footer headline and footer text.

The demo content imports a complete store: twelve handmade pieces in ceramics, textiles, candles and
woodwork (a soy candle in three scents), reviews, a welcome coupon and shipping, plus a home page, About,
Contact and a Journal.

Fonts: Fraunces, Figtree and Lora (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations.
