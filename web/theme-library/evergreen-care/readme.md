# Evergreen Care

A calm, trustworthy theme for clinics, family practices and health professionals, in soft teal and white.

## Highlights

- Info bar with opening hours, address and a tap-to-call phone number.
- Header appointment button, a split hero with reassurance badges and an "open today" card.
- Services, doctors and "first visit" step sections in the demo home page.
- An appointment band above the footer, practice details in the footer, a medical disclaimer line.
- Article layouts: cards, list with thumbnails or compact; rounded, slightly rounded or square corners.

## Customizer

- **Evergreen Colors**: accent, soft background, footer/heading color — live preview.
- **Evergreen Layout**: header layout, article layout, sidebar, corners, typography, sticky header, featured images, author, appointment band.
- **Practice Details & Hero**: info bar, phone, hours, address, appointment button label and link, hero image, kicker, title, text, badges, footer text.

## Demo content

Eight health articles in five topics, a designed home page, Services (with Appointments), Our Doctors,
About and Contact pages, main and footer menus, sidebar and footer widgets. All text and images are
original; people in the demo are fictional and portraits are abstract illustrations.
