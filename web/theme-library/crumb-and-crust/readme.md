# Crumb & Crust

A bright, appetizing recipe blog theme. Coral and butter yellow on cream, DM Serif Display headings with rounded Nunito text, sticker-style category labels and generous rounded cards.

## Highlights

- Playful hero with a blob-shaped photo, a sticker and a recipe search box
- Header search field, a colour ribbon and a scalloped hero edge
- Category tiles on the home page (`cat-tiles` columns of `cat-tile` groups)
- Recipe cards inside posts (`recipe-card` group, anchor `recipe`): timing facts (`recipe-facts`), tickable ingredients (`ingredients`), big numbered steps (`steps`) and a tip (`recipe-tip`)
- Recipe archive in two or three columns, soft, outlined or flat card styles, optional sidebar with an "about me" box

## Customizer

- **Colors & Type**: accent, highlight, page background, typefaces
- **Layout**: header layout, card style, recipes per row, sidebar, featured images
- **Home Page & Footer**: hero image, heading, text, button label and link, footer text

## Demo content

Nine tested-style recipes (lemon drizzle cake, pancakes, overnight bread, focaccia and more), each with a recipe card; a designed Home page with category tiles, Recipes, About me with a Kitchen notes sub-page, and Say hello; main and social menus, sidebar and footer widgets. All text and images are original.

Fonts: DM Serif Display and Nunito, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
