# Roast & Ritual

A warm, characterful specialty coffee roaster for Pursae Commerce: a scrolling ticker, an espresso-dark header with a
cup seal and a Cart pill (mini-cart on wide screens), a full-bleed hero with a wavy bottom edge, category tiles
framed like coffee-bag labels, oat product cards with mono prices and a hard offset shadow on hover, stamp-style
sale badges, tasting-note product pages with pill tabs and a counter-style checkout with a receipt-like summary.

**Customize › Colours & Type**: accent, crema, background and text colours; Fraunces, Zilla Slab or Albert Sans
headings; rounded or square buttons.
**Customize › Header & Store**: ticker and text, espresso or oat header, offset shadow on cards, products per row,
footer text.

The demo content imports a complete roastery: twelve products in four categories (a single origin with weight and
grind options, brewing gear, a virtual gift subscription and a grouped gift box), reviews, coupons and shipping,
plus a home page, About, Contact and a Journal.

Fonts: Fraunces, Albert Sans, IBM Plex Mono and Zilla Slab (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations.
