# Frame by Frame

A film and TV review magazine theme for WordPress Go.

- A black top bar with a red "on air" light and search, a centered marquee masthead and a film-perforated section bar.
- A letterboxed hero (black bars, a slow Ken Burns move), letterbox stills on posts, wide images and cards.
- Ticket-stub category labels, a drop cap, star ratings (`fx-rating` paragraph with `fx-stars`), credit blocks (`fx-credits-block`), verdict boxes (`fx-verdict`) and numbered watchlists (`fx-watchlist` list).
- Two-up letterbox, poster wall or reviews-list layouts; closing credits in the footer ending in "Fin."
- A `page-wide` template for festival guides and tables.

## Customizer

**Colours & Screen**: festival gold, velvet, paper, ink, screening-room dark mode, film grain, font pairing (Big Shoulders & Newsreader, Newsreader only, system fonts).
**Layout**: letterbox / poster / list, letterbox images, sidebar, stills, critic names, sticky section bar.
**Front Page & Footer**: hero still, kicker, heading, line, button label and link, top bar line, footer text.

## Demo content

Eight posts (reviews, a series, a festival dispatch, a watchlist and an essay), a designed home page, a festival guide, About with Our Critics, and Contact. All text, films and images are original.

Fonts: Big Shoulders Display and Newsreader (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted.
