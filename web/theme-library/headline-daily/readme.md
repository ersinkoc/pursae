# Headline Daily

A bold news magazine theme for Pursae. A black ticker carries the latest headlines across the top of every page, a big condensed nameplate sits over a red-and-black sections bar, and the front page packs a lead story, two side stories, a numbered **Top stories** rail, a dense story grid and a row for each section you choose.

## Features

- **Front page built from your latest posts**: lead story with summary, two side stories, a numbered rail, a three- or four-column grid, then one row per section listed in *Customize › Front Page & Layout › Front page section rows* (category slugs separated by commas, e.g. `business,science,culture`).
- **Headline ticker** with a custom label; it pauses on hover or keyboard focus and stops entirely for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
- **Three mastheads**: centered nameplate, left-aligned with tagline, or inverted black.
- **Three font pairings**: Newsroom (Oswald + Source Serif 4), Tabloid (Oswald + system sans) and Broadsheet (Source Serif 4 throughout).
- Article pages with a red section kicker, standfirst from the post excerpt, drop cap, pull quotes, topic tags and a "Most read" style sidebar.
- Sidebar right, left or off; optional sticky sections bar; author bylines and card summaries on or off.
- Black footer with a widget area, footer menu, tagline and copyright line.
- Responsive down to 360px with an accessible sections menu, visible focus states and print styles.

## Widget areas and menus

- **Sidebar** (`sidebar-1`) and **Footer** (`footer-1`).
- **Sections Menu** (`primary`, supports one level of dropdowns) and **Footer Menu** (`footer`).

## Demo content

Ten fictional news stories from the invented harbour city of Port Calder, four pages (About, Standards and corrections, Contact, Advertise), section categories, menus, widgets and Customizer values. All text and images are original.
