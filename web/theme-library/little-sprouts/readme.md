# Little Sprouts

A cheerful theme for kindergartens, nurseries and daycare centres, made for WordPress Go.

- A rainbow stripe, an optional notice bar, a sprout logo mark, menu pills that light up in four colours and a green "Book a visit" button.
- A yellow hero with bobbing shapes, a blob-shaped picture and a wavy edge; colourful programme cards by age, a daily schedule timeline in a wavy green band, parent news, parent quotes and a big yellow visit band.
- Bubbly 3D buttons, coloured dot bullets, speech-bubble quotes and wavy dividers.
- Programme cards (`ls-program ls-program--red|yellow|green|blue` with an `ls-age` paragraph), a schedule (`ls-schedule` list with `<strong>` times), panels (`ls-panel`) and callouts (`ls-callout`).
- A `page-wide` template for the programmes page.

## Customizer

**Colours & Shapes**: red, yellow, blue, green, background, wavy edges, roundness (bubbly, soft, crisp), font pairing (Fredoka & Atkinson Hyperlegible, Atkinson only, system rounded).
**News & Layout**: cards or list, sidebar, author names, sticky header.
**School Details**: notice bar, enrol button label and link, hero picture, kicker and heading, second button, footer text.

## Demo content

Eight parent news posts, a designed home page, Programmes with a Daily Schedule, About with Our Team, and Visit & Enrol. All text and illustrations are original; the nursery is fictional.

Fonts: Fredoka and Atkinson Hyperlegible (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted.
