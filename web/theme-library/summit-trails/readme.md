# Summit Trails

A rugged, map-inspired theme for hiking clubs, walking groups and outdoor adventure clubs.
Topographic contour patterns, a night-slate header with a mountain-peak mark and a Join button,
a full-bleed mountain hero with an alpine-orange stats strip, trip calendars with date blocks,
trail guides with difficulty badges (easy, moderate, hard) and route stats, numbered route
steps, gear checklists, safety notes, gear reviews with ratings and a membership call to action.
Zilla Slab headings with Work Sans text.

## Customizer

- **Trail Colors**: alpine accent, header and footer slate, page background, glacier tint (live preview).
- **Layout & Terrain**: trail log as cards, a list or compact logbook rows; sidebar right, left
  or none; standard or wide container; three typography choices; topographic contour patterns;
  sticky header; featured images; author.
- **Hero & Membership**: hero image, kicker, title, text, stats strip, second button, the Join
  button label and link (header, hero and footer) and the footer text.

## Content helpers

Demo content uses these classes, available in any post: `st-badge st-badge--easy|moderate|hard`
(difficulty pills), `st-stats` on a Columns block (route stats strip), `st-route` on a list
(numbered route steps), `st-gear` on a list (checklist), `st-note` on a Group (safety note),
`st-trips` on a list (calendar with date blocks), `st-rating` on a paragraph.

## Demo content

One click imports a designed home page (trip calendar, trail guides, club numbers, a gear
review and the membership call to action), the trip calendar, membership with an FAQ, about and
contact pages, eight trail guides, trip reports, skills and gear posts with comments, menus,
widgets and the Customizer settings. All text and artwork are original.

Fonts: Zilla Slab and Work Sans, SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted.
