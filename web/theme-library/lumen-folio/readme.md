# Lumen Folio

A dark, full-bleed showcase for photographers. The front page is a masonry wall of your stories;
every story opens on a quiet, lightbox-like stage with previous/next arrows and a close button,
followed by a narrow column of text.

**Category:** Photography · **Style:** Dark full-bleed masonry gallery

## Customizer

- **Gallery:** layout (masonry, even grid, stacked), columns (2–4), spacing (none to airy),
  titles on images (on hover, always, hidden), front page introduction, front page cover image.
- **Colors & Type:** accent, background and text colors (live preview), font pairing
  (Jost & Cormorant italic, Cormorant throughout, system sans-serif).
- **Header & Footer:** split or centered header, sticky header, date and author on stories, footer text.

## Menus and widgets

Primary Menu (with drop-down submenus), Social Links (in the footer bar) and a Footer widget area.

## Demo content

"Halden Light", a landscape photographer's journal: nine photo stories in four categories,
About (with a Workshops sub-page), Prints and Contact pages, menus, footer widgets and a few comments.
All text and images are original; the images were generated for this theme.
