# Ink & Needle

A tattoo and piercing studio theme for WordPress Go, in black and bone with a blood-red accent.

- A top bar with studio hours and a booking button, a centered blackletter masthead with a star ornament, and a menu separated by stars.
- Double-ruled frames around every image, ribbon banners for categories, a blackletter drop cap and star bullets.
- A front page with a framed hero, services, a bone-coloured flash band with price tags, the artists, journal and a booking call.
- Flash sheets (`in-flash` gallery with captions as price tags), artist cards (`in-artist`), Roman-numeral steps (`in-steps` list), notes (`in-note`) and price tables (`in-prices`).
- A `page-wide` template for artists and the flash wall.

## Customizer

**Ink & Colours**: blood red, bone, ink, black or bone page, ornaments, paper texture, font pairing (Pirata One & Spectral, Spectral only, system serif).
**Portfolio & Layout**: two or three framed columns or a framed list, sidebar, artist names.
**Studio & Booking**: booking label and link, top bar text, hero image, banner and heading, footer text.

## Demo content

Eight posts (flash day, tattoo and piercing aftercare, artist spotlights, a first-tattoo checklist, a guest artist, cover-ups), a designed home page, Artists with a Flash wall, Booking with prices, About and Contact. All text and flash artwork are original; the studio and artists are fictional.

Fonts: Pirata One and Spectral (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted.
