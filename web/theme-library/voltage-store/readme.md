# Voltage

A sharp, dark electronics store for Goocommerce. A utility bar with a live status dot, a header built around a
big product search (press <kbd>/</kbd> to focus it) with the cart total and a mini-cart, a department bar, a hero
with a glow and spec chips, a bento grid of categories, a trust strip, dark product cards whose border lights up
and whose add-to-cart button slides in on hover, spec-sheet product pages and a numbered checkout.

**Customize › Colours & Mode**: dark or light mode, accent and highlight colours, background and text (dark mode).
**Customize › Type & Shape**: Space Grotesk, Inter or JetBrains Mono headings; rounded or sharp corners; card glow.
**Customize › Header & Store**: utility bar and text, big search or minimal header, products per row (3–5),
footer text.

The demo content imports a complete gadget store: twelve products in four categories (a phone with storage and
colour options, a smartwatch with three bands, an action camera sold through a partner), reviews, coupons and
shipping, plus a home page, About, Contact and a Journal.

Fonts: Space Grotesk, Inter and JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations.
