# Aurum & Pearl

A quiet, luxurious fine-jewellery boutique for Goocommerce: a slim announcement line, a centred masthead
with a letter-spaced wordmark between search and the bag (with a mini-cart), a menu between hairline rules,
a full-bleed hero with an italic serif headline and a gold ornament, arched category frames, centred product
cards that draw a fine gold frame and reveal "add to bag" on hover, an unhurried product page, and an onyx
footer. On small screens the menu opens as a full-screen overlay.

**Customize › Colours & Type**: gold accent, champagne panels, background and text colours, font pairing
(Cormorant Garamond or Marcellus with Jost), solid or hairline buttons, Noir (dark) mode.
**Customize › Masthead & Boutique**: announcement line and its text, masthead layout (centred or inline),
category frame shape (arched, round, square), products per row, footer text.

The demo content imports a complete store: twelve pieces in rings, necklaces, earrings and bracelets (a
solitaire ring in sizes 5–8), reviews, a welcome coupon and insured shipping, plus a home page, About,
Contact and a Journal.

Fonts: Cormorant Garamond, Jost and Marcellus (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations.
