# Paws & Whiskers

A cheerful veterinary clinic and pet care theme. Warm orange and teal on cream, rounded Fredoka headings with highly legible Atkinson Hyperlegible text.

## Highlights

- Emergency line bar with a pulsing dot above the header (can be switched off)
- Front-page hero with a blob-shaped image, badges and two calls to action, wavy section edges
- Service cards with icon tiles (`feature-card`, `icon`), vet cards with round portraits (`team-card`), adoption cards (`pet-card` with `tags` marks)
- Bubbly or outlined card style, grid or list blog, sidebar on by default
- Teal footer with opening hours and an emergency call box
- Full width with banner page template (`page-full`)

## Customizer

- **Colors & Type**: Orange accent, teal, background, typefaces, wavy section edges
- **Header & Emergency Line**: Show the emergency bar above the header, emergency message, phone number, booking button label, booking link
- **Front Page Hero**: Hero image, heading, text
- **Blog & Footer**: Card style, blog layout, show the sidebar beside posts, opening hours, footer text

## Demo content

Seven advice posts (fireworks night, cat check-ups, rabbit diet, a new puppy, dental health, an adoption story, hot days), a designed Home page, Services & vets, Adoption corner, Appointments, About and Contact; main and social menus, sidebar and footer widgets. All text and images are original; the illustrations were generated for this theme.

Fonts: Atkinson Hyperlegible and Fredoka, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
