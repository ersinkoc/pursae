# Numera

A calm, clear theme for accounting firms and tax advisers: blue-green tones, Sora headings, readable tables in three styles, a deadline banner, services and pricing plans, a tax-deadline calendar page and a finance-tips blog.

Category: **finance** · Style: Calm blue-green, clear tables, friendly geometric sans

## Highlights

- A deadline banner above the header that links to the tax-calendar page
- A split hero with a floating stat card
- Pricing plans with a featured plan, and tables in three styles: striped, thin lines or boxed cells
- Finance tips as cards, a list with thumbnails or compact rows
- Header as a white bar, a teal bar or a floating rounded bar
- Responsive down to 360px with an accessible mobile menu (Escape closes it), visible focus states and `prefers-reduced-motion` support

## Customizer

**Colors & Type**: Primary (teal); Accent (mint); Soft surface; Typefaces; Table style.

**Header & Deadline Banner**: Header style; Keep the header visible while scrolling; Deadline banner; Deadline banner link; Header button label; Header button link.

**Front Page Hero**: Hero image; Small label above the heading; Heading; Text; Stat card value; Stat card label; Second link label; Second link URL.

**Blog & Footer**: Post list layout; Sidebar; Show featured images; Show the author on posts and cards; Footer text.

## Demo content
