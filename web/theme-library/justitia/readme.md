# Justitia

A dignified theme for law firms with a legal blog: deep burgundy and charcoal with gold hairlines, Caslon headings, a full-bleed hero with a free-consultation call to action, practice areas, attorney profiles, case results and articles that answer everyday law questions.

Category: **legal** · Style: Burgundy and charcoal, Caslon serif, gold hairlines

## Highlights

- A full-bleed hero with a burgundy, charcoal or light overlay and a free-consultation button
- Practice-area cards with roman numerals, attorney profiles, a case-results band and a client quote
- A consultation band above the footer with the phone number, and a legal disclaimer in the footer
- Articles as an editorial list or as cards, with a drop cap on single articles
- Header as an ivory bar, a centered crest with the menu below, or a burgundy bar
- Responsive down to 360px with an accessible mobile menu (Escape closes it), visible focus states and `prefers-reduced-motion` support

## Customizer

**Brand & Colors**: Primary (burgundy); Charcoal; Gold; Page background; Typefaces; Gold hairlines under headings and around cards.

**Header & Consultation**: Header style; Keep the header visible while scrolling; Show the charcoal strip above the header; Strip text; Phone number; Consultation button label; Consultation button link; Consultation band heading.

**Front Page Hero**: Hero image; Image overlay; Small line above the heading; Heading; Text; Second button label; Second button link.

**Articles & Footer**: Post list layout; Sidebar; Show featured images; Show the author on posts and cards; Legal disclaimer; Footer text.

## Demo content

Appearance › Themes › Import Demo Content adds 8 posts, 7 pages (Steady counsel for life's hardest questions, Articles, Practice areas, Our attorneys, Case results, About the firm, Free consultation), 7 categories, 12 images, a main menu with submenus, a footer menu, sidebar and footer widgets and the Customizer values shown in the screenshot. The front page is a static page built from blocks; the template `page-wide` is used for full-width pages. All names, firms, addresses and numbers are fictional.
