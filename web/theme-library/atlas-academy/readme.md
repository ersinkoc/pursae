# Atlas Academy

A bold, modern theme for independent online course creators. Thick ink borders, chunky offset
shadows, rotated sticker badges and violet, coral and mint colours, with Sora headings and Plus
Jakarta Sans text. An announcement bar, an Enroll button in the header, a split hero with a
highlighted word and floating stat stickers, course catalogue cards with level pills and prices,
numbered curriculum modules, student story cards, a free-lessons blog and an enroll call to action.

## Customizer

- **Academy Colors**: primary, accent, page background and ink (text, borders and shadows), all live.
- **Layout & Style**: lessons as a card grid, a list or minimal; sidebar right, left or none;
  narrow, normal or wide container; three typography choices; bold offset shadows, soft shadows
  or flat outlines; sticky header; featured images; author.
- **Hero, Enroll & Announcements**: the announcement bar and its text, the Enroll button label and
  link, the hero image, kicker, title, highlighted word, text and second button, the two hero
  stickers, the footer call to action and the footer text.

## Demo content

One click imports a designed home page (courses, curriculum, student stories, numbers, free
lessons and a call to action), Courses with a Design Basics curriculum page, About, Pricing and
Contact pages, eight free lessons and news posts with comments, menus, widgets and the
Customizer settings. All text and artwork are original.

Fonts: Sora and Plus Jakarta Sans, SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted.
