# Northstar Ventures

A quiet, confident theme for venture capital firms, angel networks and investment partnerships. Dark navy and silver with a gold highlight, a full-height hero over a night sky with an optional CSS starfield, editorial Instrument Serif headlines, a portfolio grid, a team grid and long-form thesis essays with drop caps. An ivory reading scheme is one click away.

## Highlights

- Night navy or ivory paper color scheme; logo-left or centered header
- Essays as a numbered index (“No. 01”) or a grid; drop cap and serif pull quotes on essays
- Block classes: `statement`, `numbers` + `figure`, `portfolio` (logo, name, description, `stage`), `team` (grayscale portraits that color on hover), `principles` (Roman numerals), `pitch-band`, `hairline-top`

## Customizer

- **Northstar Colors**: color scheme, accent (silver), navy, highlight (gold)
- **Northstar Layout**: font pairing, header style, essay list layout, sidebar, starfield, featured images, author names
- **Northstar Hero & Footer**: hero kicker, heading, text, link label and URL (also the header button), hero background image, footer text

## Demo content

“Halden North”, a fictional early-stage fund: a designed home page, Portfolio, Team, Approach and Contact pages, and seven essays and letters. Company names, people and logos are invented; portraits are abstract silhouettes. All text and images are original.
