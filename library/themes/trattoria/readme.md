# Trattoria

A warm Italian restaurant theme. Cream paper, terracotta and olive, Playfair Display italics and Lora text, and a layout that puts the essentials first: a full-bleed hero with a reservation button, the menu, opening hours and the way to the door.

## Highlights

- Centered masthead with phone number and reservation button, olive menu bar below (or a split layout)
- Full-bleed hero with heading, text, reservation and menu buttons, tall or medium
- Menu page styles: `menu-list` items with dotted price leaders (`dish`, `price`, `desc`, `tag`), a framed `menu-card` and `menu-section` headings
- Opening hours (`hours-list`), signature dishes (`signature-dish`) and an olive band (`band-olive`)
- Opening hours, address and phone in the footer, set in the Customizer
- News from the kitchen as two-column cards or a classic single column, with an optional sidebar

## Customizer

- **Colors & Type**: terracotta accent, olive, paper background, typefaces, linen texture
- **Header & Reservations**: header layout, reservation button label and link, phone number
- **Front Page Hero**: image, heading, text, hero height, second button label and link
- **Footer, Hours & Blog**: opening hours, address, news layout, sidebar, footer text

## Demo content

Eight news posts (seasonal menus, the oven, the wine evening, a tiramisu recipe and more), a designed Home page, a Menu with a Wine list sub-page, Our story and Reservations; main and social menus, sidebar and footer widgets. All text and images are original.
