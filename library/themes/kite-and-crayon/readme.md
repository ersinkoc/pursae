# Kite & Crayon

A bright toy shop: a header with the language switcher and the cart, a playful hero, chunky category tiles, favourites and new toys, and simple store pages in navy, mint and coral. Outfit, Figtree and Caveat, self-hosted. Ships a complete store demo: wooden toys, games, art supplies, room decor and a birthday bundle, priced in euros.

**A plain store theme.** The header, footer, pages and posts come from the theme's own templates
(templates/), the store pages from Pursae Commerce's templates; colours and type are in assets/style.css.

**Pursae Multi Language ready.** The header carries the language switcher; translate pages, products and
categories with Pursae Multi Language.

The demo imports a complete store: twelve products (with variations and a bundle), four categories,
reviews, coupons (WELCOME10) and shipping, plus a home page, Our promise and Contact made of blocks.

Fonts: Outfit, Figtree and Caveat (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations made for
this theme, dedicated to the public domain (CC0).
