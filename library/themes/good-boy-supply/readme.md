# Good Boy Supply

A cheerful pet store: a bold header with the language switcher and the cart, a sunny hero, rounded category tiles, bestsellers and new arrivals, and friendly store pages in navy and orange. Fredoka and Nunito, self-hosted. Ships a complete store demo: beds, harnesses in three sizes, cat furniture, toys, care and a puppy starter kit.

**A plain store theme.** The header, footer, pages and posts come from the theme's own templates
(templates/), the store pages from Pursae Commerce's templates; colours and type are in assets/style.css.

**Pursae Multi Language ready.** The header carries the language switcher; translate pages, products and
categories with Pursae Multi Language.

The demo imports a complete store: twelve products (with variations and a bundle), four categories,
reviews, coupons (WELCOME10) and shipping, plus a home page, Our testers and Contact made of blocks.

Fonts: Fredoka and Nunito (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations made for
this theme, dedicated to the public domain (CC0).
