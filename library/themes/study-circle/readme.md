# Study Circle

A bright, friendly forum theme for study groups, courses and learning communities, built for
**Pursae Forum**: a clean header with the forum's Log in and Join links, a welcoming hero, subject-room
tiles, a how-it-works strip, a live panel of fresh questions and forum stats, and calm forum views in
teal and sunshine yellow.

**Two ways to use it.**

- *As a theme.* Activate Study Circle: the header, footer, pages and the forum wrapper
  (`templates/forum.html`, with a sidebar of forum stats and recent topics) come from the theme's
  own templates. The theme declares `supports: {"pursae-forum": true}`, so forum views print their own
  title, breadcrumbs and actions. Customizer › Forum sets the accent colour and the forum layout
  (with a sidebar or full width).
- *With Pursae Web Builder.* Web Builder › Theme Library › Study Circle imports the header (Log in and
  Join links), footer, front page (hero and subject tiles linking the demo forums), post, page,
  archive, search and 404 templates into Hello Pursae, plus the Community guidelines and About pages.
  The header part carries the forum's colour tokens, so forum views follow the theme.

**Demo.** Importing the demo switches Pursae Forum on and adds six members (two tutors who moderate),
three categories with seven forums (Introductions, Study Tips, Mathematics, Sciences, Languages, Writing
and Humanities, Exam Prep), ten questions with answers, the Home, Community guidelines and About pages
and the menus. Remove Demo Content deletes all of it again.

No JavaScript on the front end. Fonts: Lexend and Nunito (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted.
Artwork: original illustrations made for this theme, dedicated to the public domain (CC0).
