# Dog-Ear Books

An independent bookshop for Pursae Commerce with an editorial soul. A thin strip with a note, search, account and a
basket (with a mini-basket), a newspaper masthead (a big Playfair Display title between double rules, sections in
small capitals), a bookplate hero, numbered section tiles, books standing on shelves, a folded dog-ear corner for
sale badges, staff-pick product pages with a drop cap in the description and a quiet, paper-coloured checkout.

**Customize › Colours & Type**: accent, shelf, highlight, paper and text colours; classic (Playfair Display, Crimson
Pro and Libre Franklin), bookish (Crimson Pro throughout) or modern (Playfair Display and Libre Franklin).
**Customize › Masthead & Shelves**: newspaper or compact masthead, the top strip note, shelves under the books,
books per shelf, footer text.

The demo content imports a complete bookshop with invented titles: twelve products in four sections (a linen
notebook in three colours, a virtual e-book), reviews, coupons and shipping, plus a home page, About, Contact and
a Journal.

Fonts: Playfair Display, Crimson Pro and Libre Franklin (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations.
