# Inkwell

A classic editorial blog theme for writers and essayists. Inkwell sets your words the way a good
literary journal would: a centered masthead between ornamental rules, high-contrast Playfair
Display headlines over Crimson Pro text, a generous reading measure, drop caps, small-caps meta
lines and footnote-like figure captions, all on warm ivory paper with a single deep accent.

## Features

- Centered masthead with an optional line above the title, in three styles: ornate, plain or compact.
- Sidebar on the right, on the left, or switched off for a single centered column.
- Three post lists: a classic list with a lead story, a two-column grid, or a table of contents
  with dotted leaders.
- Drop caps, numbered figure captions, asterism separators, pull quotes and styled tables.
- Four font pairings (self-hosted, no external requests), three text sizes.
- Footer widget columns, a footer menu and editable footer text.
- Accessible: skip link, visible focus rings, keyboard-friendly submenus, reduced-motion support.

## Customizer

**Inkwell colors** — accent, paper and ink colors (live preview).
**Inkwell typography** — font pairing, text size, drop cap.
**Inkwell layout** — masthead style, masthead line (and whether to show it), sidebar position,
post list style, featured images, author names, sticky menu bar, footer text.

## Demo content

"The Marginalia": eight original essays and notes on reading and writing, four pages (About,
Colophon, Reading List, Contact), two menus, sidebar and footer widgets, and twelve original
illustrations.
