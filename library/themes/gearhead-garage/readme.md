# Gearhead Garage

A bold auto repair and detailing theme. Carbon black and racing red, Chakra Petch capitals with Barlow text, angled sections and racing stripes. Switches to a light workshop mode.

## Highlights

- Dark carbon mode with an optional carbon-fibre texture, or a light workshop mode
- Racing stripes and angled edges (both switchable), skewed buttons and category tags
- Full-bleed hero with the workshop hours band underneath
- Service tables (`service-table`), price lists, stat rows (`stat-row`, `stat`, `stat-label`), feature cards with a from-price, crew cards
- Checkered footer with address, hours and a booking button
- Full width with banner page template (`page-full`)

## Customizer

- **Colors & Mode**: Racing red, carbon, color mode, carbon-fibre texture on dark surfaces, racing stripes on the header and headings, typefaces
- **Header & Booking**: Keep the header visible while scrolling, section edges, booking button label, booking link, phone number
- **Front Page Hero**: Hero image, heading, text, workshop hours
- **Blog & Footer**: Blog layout, show the sidebar beside posts, address, footer text

## Demo content

Seven tips posts (winter checks, warning lights, ceramic coating, brake pads, oil changes, a new alignment rig, the two-bucket wash), a designed Home page, Services & prices, Book a service, About and Contact; main and social menus, sidebar and footer widgets. All text and images are original; the illustrations were generated for this theme.
