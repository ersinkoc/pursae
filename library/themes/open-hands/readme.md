# Open Hands

A warm, hopeful theme for charities, community groups and nonprofits.

## Highlights

- Mission-statement hero with a hand-drawn highlight and impact numbers you type in the Customizer.
- Donate button in the header, pinned to the corner on phones; a dismissable-looking announcement bar.
- Story cards with a warm, natural or duotone image tone; featured, grid or list story layouts.
- A "give monthly" band above the green footer; program cards, ways-to-help and donation amount blocks in the demo.

## Customizer

- **Open Hands Colors**: ember, sunflower and fern — live preview.
- **Open Hands Layout**: story layout, sidebar, header, typography, image tone, sticky header, featured images, author.
- **Mission, Donate & Banner**: announcement bar text and link, donate label and link, pinned mobile button,
  hero image, kicker, mission statement and highlight, hero text, impact numbers, footer text.

## Demo content

Eight community stories, a designed home page, Our Mission, Programs, Get Involved (with Volunteer), Donate and
Contact pages, main and footer menus, sidebar and footer widgets. All text and images are original and fictional.
