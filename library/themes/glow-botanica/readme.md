# Glow Botanica

A soft, clean skincare and cosmetics store for Pursae Commerce. A floating capsule header (logo, menu, search,
account and a bag with a mini-cart), an inset rounded hero with a frosted cream card, round "shop by ritual"
category bubbles, blush product tiles whose add-to-cart button rises on hover, arch-framed product photos,
pill-shaped tabs and an airy checkout with a soft order summary.

**Customize › Colours & Type**: accent, tile, background and text colours; Cormorant Garamond, Marcellus or Jost
headings; pill, soft or square buttons.
**Customize › Header & Store**: announcement bar and text, capsule or full-width header, tinted or outlined
product tiles, products per row, footer text.

The demo content imports a complete beauty store: twelve products in four categories (a lipstick in three
shades, a grouped morning ritual set, sale items), reviews, a welcome coupon and shipping, plus a home page,
About, Contact and a Journal with three notes.

Fonts: Cormorant Garamond, Marcellus and Jost (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations.
