# Ledger & Co.

A trustworthy corporate theme for consultancies, accountants and advisory firms. Navy and slate with a brass accent, Source Serif headings over IBM Plex Sans, and a layout built around clear sections: a split hero, service cards, a figures band, client wordmarks, an engagement process and an Insights blog.

## Highlights

- Split hero on a static front page (image, kicker, heading, text and two buttons from the Customizer)
- Contact strip above the header and a header call-to-action button
- Three header styles: white bar, navy bar, centered logo with the menu below
- Insights blog as a card grid or a list with thumbnails, sidebar left, right or off
- Block styles for service cards (`service-card`), a navy figures band (`figures` + `figure-num`), client wordmarks (`client-logos`), numbered steps (`steps`) and testimonials (`testimonial`)
- Footer with the site description and a widget area, plus a footer menu

## Customizer

- **Brand & Colors**: primary (navy), accent (brass), section tint, typefaces
- **Header**: header style, sticky header, contact strip on/off and its text, header button label and link
- **Front Page Hero**: image, small kicker line, heading, text, button label and link
- **Blog & Footer**: post list layout, sidebar position, featured images, author byline, footer text

## Demo content

Nine articles across Strategy, Finance, Operations, Leadership and Firm News; Home, Insights, Services, About, Careers and Contact pages; a main menu with submenus, a footer menu and sidebar and footer widgets. All text and images are original.

Fonts: IBM Plex Sans and Source Serif 4, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
