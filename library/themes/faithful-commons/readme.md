# Faithful Commons

A warm, welcoming theme for places of worship and faith communities of any tradition. Gentle
limestone and gold, inscriptional Marcellus headings with humanist Alegreya Sans text, arched
windows and image tops, a service-times bar, reflection and sermon-note cards, event lists, a
volunteer call to action and an invitation band in the footer.

## Customizer

- **Stone & Gold Colors**: gold accent, page background, text color, footer and service bar color (live preview).
- **Layout & Type**: centered or split header; reflections as arched cards, a list or text only;
  sidebar right, left or none; three typography choices; arched image tops; sticky header;
  featured images; who shared each post.
- **Welcome & Gatherings**: service times bar and its text, the visit button, the hero image
  (an arched window), kicker, title, text and second button, the footer invitation and footer text.

## Demo content

One click imports a designed home page (gathering times, story, events, latest reflections and a
volunteer call to action), Our Story, Plan Your Visit, Get Involved and Contact pages, eight
reflections and news posts with comments, menus, sidebar and footer widgets and the Customizer
settings. All text and artwork are original.

Fonts: Marcellus and Alegreya Sans, SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted.
