# Campus Commons

A friendly academic theme for schools, colleges and online course providers, in navy and mustard.

## Highlights

- Notice bar with utility links, a crest-style logo mark, a navy navigation bar with search.
- Grid-paper hero with a course search box and a pinned photo.
- News as a bulletin board with date badges and pins, a card grid or a simple list.
- Course cards, faculty portraits, numbers and events tables in the demo; an "apply now" band above the footer.

## Customizer

- **Campus Colors**: school color, accent, page background — live preview.
- **Campus Layout**: header, news layout, sidebar, container width, typography, sticky menu bar, featured images, author.
- **Notices, Hero & Admissions**: notice bar and text, apply label and link, hero image, kicker, title, text, course search, footer text.

## Demo content

Eight news posts in five sections, a designed home page, Courses, Events, Faculty, Admissions (with Apply), About and
Contact pages, main, utility and footer menus, sidebar and footer widgets. All people and places are fictional; all
text and images are original.
