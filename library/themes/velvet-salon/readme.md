# Velvet Salon

An elegant hair and beauty salon theme. Dusty rose and champagne, Marcellus capitals with Mulish text, arch-shaped photographs and generous white space.

## Highlights

- Top bar with hours and phone, header with a booking button (inline or centered layout, optional sticky header)
- Split front-page hero with an arched image, gold outline ring, booking button and phone
- Service price lists (`price-list`: `<strong>` name, `price` and `desc` spans), feature cards, stylist cards (`team-card`, `role`)
- Image shape switch: arches, soft rounded or square, applied to the hero, journal cards, portraits and galleries
- Beauty journal as centered cards or a list, optional sidebar, drop caps on single posts
- Booking band, opening hours and address in the footer
- Full width with banner page template (`page-full`) that uses the featured image behind the title

## Customizer

- **Colors & Type**: Rose accent, champagne, text color, typefaces, image shape
- **Header & Booking**: Header layout, keep the header visible while scrolling, booking button label, booking link, phone number, top bar line
- **Front Page Hero**: Hero image, heading, text
- **Journal & Footer**: Journal layout, show the sidebar beside journal posts, opening hours, address, footer text

## Demo content

Seven journal posts (blow-dry tips, balayage or highlights, autumn colours, scalp care, lash lifts, a new stylist, a wedding-morning timeline), a designed Home page, Services & prices with a Consultations sub-page, About with the stylists, Book and Contact; main and social menus, sidebar and footer widgets. All text and images are original; the illustrations were generated for this theme.
