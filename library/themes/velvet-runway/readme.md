# Velvet Runway

An elegant fashion and lifestyle magazine. A grand centred serif masthead with an issue line, a
cover story with an editorial mosaic below it, and articles with a centred italic headline,
standfirst, drop cap and pull quotes, in a muted palette of ivory, espresso and oxblood. An
optional evening edition turns the whole magazine dark.

**Category:** Fashion · **Style:** Editorial serif magazine, muted luxury

## Customizer

- **Colours & Type:** accent, paper and ink colours (live preview), evening edition (dark mode),
  font pairing (Bodoni Moda & Karla, Fraunces & Karla, system serif).
- **Masthead & Layout:** masthead (grand, compact, left aligned), front page layout (cover story
  and mosaic, even grid, long list), drop cap, bylines, issue line, footer text.

## Demo content

"The Velvet Edit": eight stories in Runway, Style, Beauty and Culture, About with a Masthead
sub-page, Advertise and Contact, menus, footer widgets and comments. Text and images are original.
