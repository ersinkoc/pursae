# Green Basket

A friendly organic grocery and farm shop for Pursae Commerce: a delivery-note strip, a rounded header with a
big product search and a cart pill showing the basket total (with a mini-cart), a leaf-green aisle bar
with pill links, a rounded hero with a butter card and badges, round category bubbles, cheerful product
cards with sticker sale badges and an add button that is always ready, and a soil-brown footer with a
scalloped edge. On small screens the aisles open as a drop-down panel and the search moves behind an icon.

**Customize › Colours & Type**: leaf accent, tomato sale colour, butter panels, background and text
colours, font pairing (Fredoka or Young Serif with Nunito), round or softly rounded corners.
**Customize › Header & Store**: delivery note and its text, the big header search, products per row,
footer text.

The demo content imports a complete store: twelve products in fruit and veg, dairy and eggs, bakery and
pantry (apples by the kilo, a weekly veg box), reviews, coupons and local delivery, plus a home page,
About, Contact and a Journal.

Fonts: Fredoka, Nunito and Young Serif (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations.
