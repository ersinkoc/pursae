# AppNest

A friendly marketing site for a mobile app. The hero puts your screenshot inside a CSS phone mockup next to app store buttons and a rating line; the front page continues with feature chips, an alternating feature tour, a row of phone screens, reviews, pricing and an FAQ; the blog carries release notes. Outfit is self-hosted.

## Highlights

- CSS phone mockup in three finishes (midnight, silver, brand color); `phone-frame` on any image block turns it into a phone screen
- App Store and Google Play buttons in the hero and in the footer's download panel
- Frosted-glass or solid header; soft, bubbly or crisp corners
- Block classes: `chips` + `chip-icon`, `tour` + `step`, `phone-row`, `quotes`, `plans` + `plan--pro`, `faq` (styled accordions), `version-list`, `release-pill`
- Blog as cards or a list with thumbnails

## Customizer

- **AppNest Colors**: brand color, second color, background tint, phone finish
- **AppNest Layout**: header style, corner roundness, font, blog layout, sidebar, featured images, author names
- **AppNest App & Hero**: store buttons on/off and their links, header button, hero kicker, heading, text, rating line, phone screen image, footer text

## Demo content

“Nestly”, a fictional calm planner app: a designed home page, Pricing, Help & FAQ, About and Contact, and seven posts with release notes, tips and design notes. All text and images, including the app screens, are original.
