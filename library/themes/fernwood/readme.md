# Fernwood

A leafy plant shop: a split header with the language switcher and the cart, an editorial hero, category tiles, featured and new plants, and calm store pages in forest green and terracotta. Fraunces and Albert Sans, self-hosted. Ships a complete store demo: houseplants in three sizes, planters, care tools and a gift box.

**A plain store theme.** The header, footer, pages and posts come from the theme's own templates
(templates/), the store pages from Pursae Commerce's templates; colours and type are in assets/style.css.

**Pursae Multi Language ready.** The header carries the language switcher; translate pages, products and
categories with Pursae Multi Language.

The demo imports a complete store: twelve products (with variations and a bundle), four categories,
reviews, coupons (WELCOME10) and shipping, plus a home page, Our greenhouse and Contact made of blocks.

Fonts: Fraunces and Albert Sans (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted. Artwork: original illustrations made for
this theme, dedicated to the public domain (CC0).
