# Green Pulse

A climate and environmental news magazine. A lime "live pulse" ticker sits over a deep forest
header with a heartbeat-line logo mark; bold Epilogue headlines meet Public Sans text; lime
marker highlights, a magazine grid that leads with the top story, data callouts with huge
numbers, numbered action guides, campaign bands, a newsletter signup and an optional dark mode.

## Customizer

- **Pulse Colors**: forest, lime highlight, leaf links and labels, page background (live
  preview), and a dark "forest night" mode.
- **Newsroom Layout**: magazine (lead story large), even grid or list; sidebar right, left or
  none; narrow, normal or wide container; three typography choices; sticky header; featured
  images; bylines.
- **Ticker, Lead Story & Newsletter**: show the ticker and its text, the lead image, kicker,
  headline, text and button, the newsletter band title, text and button, and the footer text.

## Block styles for content

Add these class names in the block editor (Advanced › Additional CSS class):
`gp-stat` on a group (data callout with a big number in a `gp-big` paragraph), `gp-kicker`
on a paragraph, `gp-guides` on columns (numbered cards), `gp-numbers` on columns inside a
`gp-section gp-section--forest` group, `gp-campaign` on a group (lime campaign band).

## Demo content

One click imports a designed front page (top story, numbers band, latest stories, action
guides and a campaign band), About, Take Action and Contact pages, eight stories with data
tables, callouts and comments, menus, sidebar and footer widgets and the Customizer settings.
All text and artwork are original.

Fonts: Epilogue and Public Sans, SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted.
