# Studio Fizz

A bold, colourful theme for creative agencies. A rounded pill header with a call-to-action button,
a big hero with a morphing image blob and floating shapes, chunky outlined cards with offset
shadows, and a static home page with services, a client marquee, numbers, team, testimonials and
a call-to-action band. Case studies are posts.

**Category:** Agency · **Style:** Bold, colourful and playful

## Customizer

- **Colours & Type:** main, second, pop and background colours (live preview), font pairing
  (Bricolage Grotesque & DM Sans, DM Sans throughout, system).
- **Shapes & Layout:** corners (extra round, soft, square), offset shadows, floating shapes,
  case study list (grid or zig-zag), sticky header, show the author.
- **Hero & Buttons:** hero heading, hero text, hero image, button label and link (header, hero and
  footer), footer text.

## Designed sections

Group blocks with these classes are styled on the home page: `fz-section`, `fz-services`
(columns become tilted colour cards), `fz-marquee` (a scrolling list), `fz-stats`, `fz-team`,
`fz-quotes` (speech bubbles) and `fz-band` (full-width call to action). Paragraphs with `fz-kicker`
become small pills.

## Demo content

"Fizzwick", a brand and digital studio: seven case studies and studio notes, a designed Home page,
Case studies (posts page), About with a Services sub-page, and Contact. Text and images are original.
